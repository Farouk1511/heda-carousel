# Heda — Meta Ads Campaign Plan (Ottawa Launch)

> **Rule #1: We do not sell features. We sell feelings.**
> Nobody downloads "swipe-based gym partner matching." People download the end of gym loneliness, the thrill of being chased on a leaderboard, and the pride of a streak that other people can see.

---

## 1. The Emotional Inventory (feature → feeling)

Every ad must be traceable to one of these. If an ad leads with a feature, it's wrong.

| What the app does | What we actually sell | The feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Swipe matching by gym/goals/intensity | "You'll never walk in alone again" | **Belonging** |
| Leaderboards (steps, streaks, weekly) | "Someone finally sees your effort" / "Someone's coming for your spot" | **Recognition / Rivalry** |
| Streak tracking | "A chain you'd be ashamed to break in front of people" | **Pride / Accountability** |
| Gym-location profiles | "The regulars become your people" | **Community / Home** |
| Fitness-first profiles (PRs, training style) | "Be known for your grind, not your looks" | **Identity** |
| Messaging | "Someone expects you at 6am" | **Obligation (the good kind)** |

Core brand promise (from the content system): **"Find your people."**
Core enemy: **training alone / the invisible quit** ("nobody notices when you stop").

---

## 2. Audience & Market

- **Geo:** Ottawa + ~25km radius (launch city). Expand ring only when gym-level density is proven.
- **Age:** 18–40 core (test 18–29 vs 25–40 as separate cells later, not day 1).
- **Who:** People who already train (or restarted 3+ times). This is NOT a "get fit" app — it's for people who already identify (or want to identify) as gym people. The content voice is tribal: "built by gym rats, for gym rats."
- **Language:** English (add FR-CA variant test in phase 2 — Ottawa is bilingual, this is a real unlock competitors will miss).
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements, but creative built 9:16-first (Reels/Stories is where this audience lives). Feed gets 1:1/4:5 crops.

---

## 3. Campaign Architecture

Three campaigns. Clean separation of jobs: **Test → Scale → Retarget.**

```
[C1] HEDA_TEST     — App Installs, ABO, 1 ad set per angle (6 ad sets)
[C2] HEDA_SCALE    — Advantage+ App Campaign (AAC), winners only
[C3] HEDA_RETARGET — Engagers + installed-not-registered
```

### C1 — Angle Testing (ABO, manual app-install campaign)
- **Objective:** App Promotion (manual setup, not Advantage+ — we need per-angle readouts).
- **Structure:** 6 ad sets = 6 emotional angles (Section 4). 2–3 ads per ad set.
- **Targeting:** Broad within geo/age. Do NOT stack interests in the test — let creative do the targeting (an ad about gym loneliness self-selects gym people). One optional 7th ad set: interest stack (gym, bodybuilding, powerlifting, GymShark, MyFitnessPal) as a control cell.
- **Budget:** $15–20/day per ad set (~$100–140/day total). Each ad set needs ~$100–150 spent before judging.
- **Kill rule:** After ~$120 spend per ad set — kill if CPI > 2× best performer OR hook rate < 20%.
- **Duration:** 10–14 days.

### C2 — Scaling (Advantage+ App Campaign)
- Take the 2–3 winning angles from C1, put 4–6 best ads into one AAC.
- Meta's algo optimizes freely. Feed it new creative variants weekly (same winning angle, new hook/visual).
- **Budget:** Start at 2× the combined winning ad sets' spend, increase ≤20%/day.
- **Optimize for:** App installs first; switch to app events (registration complete / gym selected) once you have ~50+ conversions/week flowing through the SDK.

### C3 — Retargeting (small, always-on)
- **Audiences:** IG/FB engagers 30d, video viewers 50%+, installed-but-not-registered (via SDK event exclusion).
- **Job:** Close the loop. Show social proof + "your gym is filling up" urgency.
- **Budget:** $5–10/day. This is a scalpel, not a hammer.

## 4. The Six Angles (= C1 ad sets)

Each angle is one feeling, one enemy, one promise. Copy below is launch-ready; formats specified per ad.

---

### Angle 1 — "The Loneliest Crowded Room" (Belonging)
*Enemy: gym loneliness. Feeling: finally being let in.*

**Hooks (first 3 seconds / first line):**
- "The gym is full of people and completely lonely."
- "Headphones in. Eyes down. Three years at the same gym. Zero friends."
- "The most social place in the city where nobody talks."

**Primary text:**
> You see the same faces every day. Same regulars, same nods, same silence. You share everything with them except a single conversation. That's not a you problem — it's a design problem. Nobody built the way in. Heda is the introduction you've been waiting for. See who trains at your gym. Match with people at your level. Walk in Monday actually knowing someone.

**Headlines:** "Your gym, but you know people" / "End the lonely gym era" / "Find your people"
**CTA:** Download

**Creative:**
- **Reel (9:16, 15–20s):** POV walking into a busy gym, everyone isolated in headphones → phone lifts → overlay pins appear over people ("Sarah — 47-day streak — looking for a partner"). Loneliness → connection in one visual beat.
- **Static (1:1):** Wide shot of a packed gym, everyone alone. Big type: *"Full of people. Completely lonely."* Small: "Heda fixes this."
- **Carousel:** Reuse the day-26 carousel ("The loneliest place is a crowded gym") — it's already written for this angle.

---

### Angle 2 — "Nobody Notices When You Quit" (Accountability / the invisible quit)
*Enemy: the silent week-3 disappearance. Feeling: being missed.*

**Hooks:**
- "80% of people quit the gym by week 3. Here's the real reason."
- "You didn't quit because you're lazy. You quit because nobody noticed."
- "Week 1: fired up. Week 2: life hits. Week 3: nobody notices you stopped."

**Primary text:**
> Not your alarm. Not your playlist. Not your pre-workout. You quit because no one cares if you don't show up. What if someone did? A partner who texts "where were you?" A rival watching your streak. A leaderboard that makes skipping visible. Heda makes quitting loud — which is exactly why you won't.

**Headlines:** "What if someone noticed?" / "Make quitting visible" / "Accountability that actually works"
**CTA:** Download

**Creative:**
- **Reel (15s):** Calendar UI — green checkmarks stacking, then greyed days appearing... then a phone notification slides in: *"Marcus noticed your streak is at risk 👀"* — checkmark returns. Text overlay: "The difference is people."
- **Static:** A streak counter at "21" with a crack through it. *"Nobody saw it break. That's why it broke."*
- **Talking-head UGC style (test):** "I've restarted the gym 6 times. The only time it stuck was when someone was expecting me. Here's what changed…"

---

### Angle 3 — "The Rival Effect" (Ego / Competition)
*Enemy: comfortable coasting. Feeling: the electric thrill of being chased.*

**Hooks:**
- "You lift heavier when someone's watching. Science knows why."
- "Messi had Ronaldo. Ali had Frazier. You have… your alarm clock."
- "You don't care about 10,000 steps. Until your friend hits 12,000."

**Primary text:**
> Every great athlete has a rival. Not a supporter — a rival. Someone whose numbers you check. Someone chasing yours. When somebody's coming for your spot on the leaderboard, you don't skip days. Heda gives you rivals at your gym, at your level, on a live leaderboard. Friendly rivalry. Serious results.

**Headlines:** "Find your rival" / "Someone's chasing your numbers" / "Stop competing in your head"
**CTA:** Download

**Creative:**
- **Reel (15s):** Split screen — two people's days (5am alarm vs snooze… gym vs couch) with a live leaderboard overlay showing positions swapping. Ends on rank overtake + notification: *"Alex just passed you."* Hard cut: "Your move."
- **Static:** Leaderboard UI crop, viewer at #2, tiny gap to #1. *"37 steps behind. Sleep on that."*
- **Engagement bait variant (for retargeting/organic):** "Tag someone who needs a gym rival. Not a supporter. A rival."

### Angle 4 — "A Streak Nobody Sees Is a Private Hobby" (Recognition / Pride)
*Enemy: invisible effort. Feeling: finally being seen for the grind.*

**Hooks:**
- "A 30-day streak nobody sees is just a private hobby."
- "Instagram shows your highlights. Nothing shows your grind."
- "You've been consistent for months. Nobody knows. Nobody cares. It's quietly demoralizing."

**Primary text:**
> The 5am alarms. The Tuesday leg days. The showing up when you didn't feel like it. Effort without a witness loses its power. On Heda, your consistency is public — streaks, stats, rank. People see the work, not just the results. Make your effort count for something.

**Headlines:** "Make your effort visible" / "Where effort is seen" / "Show the work"
**CTA:** Download

**Creative:**
- **Reel (15s):** Montage of unglamorous effort (dark morning, chalk, last rep) — each clip stamps a day onto a growing public streak counter with reactions/fire emojis appearing from other users. "Seen. Finally."
- **Static:** Two phones side by side. IG grid (posed photo) vs Heda profile (streak 47, rank #3, gym name). *"One shows your angles. One shows your grind."*

### Angle 5 — "Your Gym Has a Hidden Community" (Curiosity / FOMO)
*Enemy: coincidence without connection. Feeling: discovering what was there all along.*

**Hooks:**
- "There's a community at your gym you've never seen."
- "Someone at your gym is looking for a partner right now. You just haven't met."
- "You train next to the same 30 people every week. You know zero of them."

**Primary text:**
> Same place, same time, same days as dozens of people — and you've never connected, because proximity without introduction is just coincidence. Heda is the missing layer: see who actually trains where you train, match with the ones who move like you, and turn your gym into your crew. They're already there. Go find them.

**Headlines:** "See who's at your gym" / "Your crew is already there" / "One match away"
**CTA:** Download

**Creative:**
- **Reel (15s):** Time-lapse of a gym floor; freeze-frame — profile cards fade in above regulars one by one ("bench 225 — mornings — looking for a partner"). "They were here the whole time."
- **Static (geo-personalized):** Map pin on Ottawa. *"__ people at Ottawa gyms are already on Heda."* (Update the number weekly — real numbers only.)
- **This is the strongest LAUNCH-MOMENT angle** — pairs with "be the first at your gym" urgency for early adopters.

**Hooks:**

It's the social layer for gym culture: your people, your rivals, your gym. Built by lifters, for lifters.

**Headlines:** "Built for gym rats, by gym rats" / "Not another fitness app" / "Claim your identity"
**CTA:** Download

**Creative:**

## 5. Creative Production Matrix

Minimum viable launch: **18 ads** (6 angles × 3 formats). Priority order:

| Priority | Asset | Angles | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | 6 statics (1:1 + 9:16 crops) | all | Carousel/marketing tool — brand type on purple, leaderboard UI crops |
| P0 | 3 UGC-style reels | 1, 2, 3 | Phone-shot in a real Ottawa gym, native feel, captions burned in |
| P1 | 3 UI-demo reels | 3, 4, 5 | Screen recordings + motion type (leaderboard overtake is the hero clip) |
| P1 | 2 carousels | 1, 6 | Already written in the content system (day 26, day 43) — re-export at 1080×1350 |
| P2 | 1 manifesto reel | 6 | Higher production, doubles as launch film |

Creative specs: 9:16 (1080×1920) master for video, safe zones for Reels UI; statics at 1080×1350 (4:5) + 1080×1080. Hook in first 2s, captions always (85% watch muted), logo + CTA by second 5.

## 6. Measurement

**Setup (before spending $1):**
- Meta SDK / MMP (AppsFlyer or Adjust) installed, SKAdNetwork configured for iOS.
- Events: `install` → `registration_complete` → `gym_selected` → `first_match` → `joined_leaderboard`. The real north star is **gym_selected** (a user without a gym gets zero product value).
- Standard event mapping in Events Manager; conversion schema prioritizing registration.

**KPI ladder:**

| Metric | Target (test) | Kill threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s views / impressions) | > 30% | < 20% |
| CTR (link) | > 1% | < 0.6% |
| CPI | < $4 CAD | > $8 |
| Install → registration | > 60% | < 35% |
| Cost per gym_selected | < $10 | > $20 |

**Weekly rhythm:** Mondays — kill losers, promote winners to C2, brief 2 new creative variants of the best angle. Never edit a winning ad (resets learning) — duplicate and iterate.

## 7. Budget & Phasing (first 6 weeks, CAD)

| Phase | Weeks | Spend | What's running |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test | 1–2 | ~$100–140/day | C1: all 6 angles, ABO |
| Consolidate | 3–4 | ~$150/day | C1 trimmed to top 3 + C2 launches with winners + C3 on |
| Scale | 5–6 | $200+/day (+≤20%/day) | C2 primary, C1 becomes perpetual new-angle incubator, C3 always-on |

~$6–8k total for the 6 weeks. If CPI holds under $4, that's 1,500–2,000 installs concentrated in one city — enough density for the product's network effect to start doing the marketing for you.

## 9. What NOT to do

- ❌ Never lead with "swipe to match" — Meta will read it as dating, users will read it as Tinder, and review may flag it. The day-33 carousel ("Gym Tinder? No.") exists for exactly this — keep matching language to "find gym partners."
- ❌ No body-transformation imagery or before/afters — Meta personal-attributes/health policy risk, and it's off-brand (day 21: "gym culture doesn't have a body type").
- ❌ No "you're out of shape" negative framing — policy risk (personal attributes) and wrong feeling. The enemy is *loneliness*, never the user's body.
- ❌ Don't broaden geo before Ottawa density is real. An empty leaderboard is the product's worst first impression — every ad dollar outside the launch ring makes the product feel dead.
- ❌ Don't run all 6 angles forever. By week 4 you should be riding 2–3 proven feelings hard.
