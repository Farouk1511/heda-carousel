# Heda Month 7: The Consistency Playbook

## Editorial decision

The product data guides what problems deserve attention, but the audience should not be asked to care about Heda's internal numbers.

Every Month 7 post must provide a useful idea, script, checklist, or behavior change that works without the app. Heda appears only as an optional way to make the solution easier.

## Required post structure

Every five-slide carousel follows the same sequence:

1. **Hook:** A sharp claim, tension, or curiosity gap.
2. **Problem:** A specific behavior the reader recognizes in themselves.
3. **Value:** A useful principle, framework, checklist, or reframe.
4. **Solution:** An action the reader can take immediately without Heda.
5. **CTA:** Save, share, try, message someone, or optionally use Heda.

The solution cannot be “download Heda.” If removing the product name makes the post useless, the post is not ready.

## Monthly pillars

| Week | Pillar | Reader outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose Better Training Partners | Identify compatibility, ask clearly, and avoid depending on one person |
| 2 | Build Accountability That Works | Use better check-ins, recovery rules, and weekly systems |
| 3 | Turn Your Gym Into a Community | Start conversations and build familiarity without forced networking |
| 4 | Use Competition Without Burning Out | Choose healthy rivals, compete on attendance, and protect recovery |

## Content promise

The account should become known for practical social-fitness advice:

- Copyable messages instead of vague inspiration.
- Checklists instead of product claims.
- Small behavioral systems instead of “be more disciplined.”
- Gym social skills instead of generic motivational quotes.
- Healthy accountability instead of shame.
- Sustainable rivalry instead of obsessive comparison.

## Tone

- Direct, observant, and useful.
- Specific enough to try today.
- Confident without inventing research or statistics.
- Lightly playful when describing familiar gym behavior.
- Never preachy, manipulative, or overly promotional.

## Heda placement

Heda should appear in no more than one slide per carousel, normally the final slide. Product mentions should use one of these roles:

- An optional place to find a compatible partner.
- An optional way to maintain accountability.
- An optional place to compare steps or streaks.
- An optional tool for people who do not already have a gym community.

Avoid internal milestones, database totals, vanity metrics, or “look how fast we are growing” posts unless a genuine public milestone deserves a separate announcement.

## Format mix

- **Carousels:** frameworks, scripts, checklists, and reframes.
- **Reels:** demonstrate one script or social situation in under 15 seconds.
- **Stories:** polls, question boxes, and accountability prompts.
- **Member reposts:** proof that the advice is being used, only with consent.

## Success measures

Judge the content first by saves, shares, profile visits, and meaningful comments. Then connect each topic to the appropriate product behavior behind the scenes:

- Partner advice → discovery and partner requests.
- Accountability advice → repeat app activity and step sync.
- Gym-community advice → invitations and home-gym density.
- Competition advice → leaderboard use and shared stats.

Product analytics select future topics; they do not become the topic by default.
